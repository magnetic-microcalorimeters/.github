# magnetic-microcalorimeters

Open-source software organization for the development of **magnetic microcalorimeters (MMC)**.
An MMC is a cryogenic particle detector operated at a few tens of millikelvin. The energy of an
absorbed photon or particle heats a paramagnetic sensor in a weak magnetic field and changes its
magnetization. A SQUID reads out this magnetization change. An MMC combines eV-scale energy
resolution with a fast and nearly linear response. The aim is to support the whole development
of an MMC, from its design and simulation to its operation and data analysis.
